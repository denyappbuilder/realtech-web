# REALTECH CZ
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product purpose and evidence
Czech technology and AI publication and YouTube channel by Daniel and Sam. Implemented product truth is in `src/pages/o-nas.astro`; published articles and their existing covers are in `src/content/clanky` and `public/images`. Headlines, prose, statistics, links, author attribution and media associations are not design material to invent or replace.

## Brand commitments
The owner explicitly requested a substantial premium redesign, with Apple's restraint and craft as the reference, not a clone. Preserve the REALTECH wordmark, recognizable red accent and factual editorial content. Reading and discovery take precedence over promotional UI. No additional direction interview is needed: this brief is settled.

## Capabilities and constraints
Astro static pages plus existing Cloudflare Pages archive filtering. Preserve server-rendered content, no-JS query filtering, progressive search and retry/reset states, accessible reading outline/focus, theme controls, existing responsive images and metadata. No new accounts, services, tracking, backend or dependency changes in this design branch. Production remains untouched until the owner approves a finished preview.

## Operating context
Inferred scene for design decisions: Czech readers moving between a phone in daylight and a larger screen in the evening; light and dark are equally supported. This is a design assumption, not audience research.
